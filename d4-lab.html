Rubik's Cube Face: D₄ Symmetry Lab

A single face of a Rubik's Cube is a square. Its four rotations and four reflections form the dihedral group D₄. Use this lab to explore those eight symmetries.

Current transformation

The 8 elements of D₄

Rotations: e, r, r², r³ = 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°.

Reflections: s, sr, sr², sr³ = four mirror symmetries.

D₄ has 8 elements. It is non-abelian because rotation and reflection do not always commute.

Group rules

r⁴ = e
s² = e
sr = r⁻¹s

Four quarter-turns return the face to its start. Two reflections also return it to its start.

Important mathematical note

The D₄ model here describes the symmetries of one square face of a Rubik's Cube. It does not model every possible move or the full symmetry group of the entire cube. A physical Rubik's Cube turn changes the positions of smaller cubelets; this page demonstrates square-face symmetry only.
