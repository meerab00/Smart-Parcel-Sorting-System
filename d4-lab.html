Eight elements of D₄

Rotations: e, r, r², r³ (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°).

Reflections: s, sr, sr², sr³ (four mirror symmetries).

The group has 8 elements and is non-abelian: the order of a rotation and reflection can change the result.

A reflection performed twice returns the parcel to its original mirror state.

Why this matters

A square-faced parcel can be modeled by D₄ when both turning and flipping are allowed. The main ParcelFlow batch currently calculates quarter-turn orientation with ℤ₄; this page is a separate interactive D₄ demonstration and does not claim to control a physical parcel.
